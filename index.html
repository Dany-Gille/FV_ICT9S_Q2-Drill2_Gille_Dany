OPERATION BROTHERHOOD MONTESSORI CENTER

At Operation Brotherhood Montessori Center, each child is nurtured in the holistic method of Dr. Maria Montessori.

Welcome to the O.B. Montessori Center in the Philippines! 

We are a forward-thinking pre-kindergarten through grade 12 co-educational institution, providing a strong alternative to international schools.

At OBMC, we combine time-tested traditions with modern innovations to equip students for real-world success. 

Our mission is to guide learners into self-reliant, capable individuals who can lead, adapt, and make a positive impact in a rapidly changing world. 

Discover our academic programs and experience the dynamic community that makes OBMC a special place for a child to learn and thrive.

Campuses



Fairview
Our Fairview Campus in Quezon City gives quality education to its thriving community of young families.
Greenhills
Our Greenhills Campus in San Juan City offers its students a creative and environment to influence their imagination, love of learning, and service to the community.
Sta. Ana
Our Sta. Ana Campus is known for its strong academic programs, community involvement, and love for culture and heritage.
Angeles
Our Angeles Campus, located in the vibrant province Pampanga, provides a nurturing dynamic Montessori environment for students to thrive personally and academically.
Las Piñas
Our Las Piñas Campus pays respect to it's cultural history, the birthplace of the Sarao Jeepney.Its welcoming atmosphere promotes development on students.
